What data we collect We only collect data you actively provide: - Account: email address and a hashed password (handled by Supabase Auth — we never see your plaintext password). - Profile: optional display name, language preference, onboarding answers (goals, experience level). - Your peptide stack: peptide name (from our public catalog), dosage, frequency, personal notes you choose to add. - Dose log entries: timestamp, dose taken, optional notes you write. - Progress measurements: your body weight, and your own 1-5 ratings for sleep, energy and recovery, each attached to a calendar date, plus an optional note you write. - Vial records: the vial strength in milligrams, the amount of bacteriostatic water you entered, the date you opened the vial, and the discard-after period you chose. - Cycle records: a cycle start date, the number of weeks on, and the number of weeks off. - Reminder settings: the time of day and the weekdays you chose for each dose reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone by iOS — we do not operate a push notification server and we do not collect a push notification token. - Subscription state: whether you have an active premium entitlement, the product identifier, and expiration date — synced from RevenueCat. We do not store payment information; that stays with Apple. - Account lifecycle: account creation date and, if you request deletion, the deletion-requested timestamp. Everything above is information you type in yourself. We do not use third-party analytics (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel). We do not show ads. We do not integrate Apple HealthKit and we do not read anything from your device's Health app. We do not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera. 3. How we use your data - To authenticate you and keep your stack in sync across devices. - To gate access to premium features based on your subscription state. - To draw your progress charts and to work out how much is left in an open vial. - To honour App Store and GDPR rights (access, deletion). We do not sell, rent, or share your data with marketers. 4. Where your data is stored Personal data is stored in Supabase (Postgres database, EU region — Frankfurt, Germany), encrypted in transit and at rest, and readable only by you. Subscription state is processed by RevenueCat (purchase metadata only; no personally identifiable information beyond the user identifier we generate). Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. 5. Third parties we rely on - Supabase (Supabase Inc.) — database & authentication. supabase.com/privacy - RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.) — subscription state management. revenuecat.com/privacy - Apple — App Store distribution and in-app purchase processing. apple.com/legal/privacy 6. Your rights (GDPR) If you are in the EU/EEA you have the right to: - Access the personal data we hold about you. - Request correction of inaccurate data. - Request deletion of your account and data ("right to be forgotten"). - Receive an export of your data in a machine-readable format. - Object to or restrict processing. - Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. You can exercise the deletion right at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. Your account is deactivated immediately and signs you out. After 30 days, all of your personal data is permanently and irreversibly purged from our database. If you sign in within those 30 days, you can restore the account. For any other request, write to the contact email above. We respond within 30 days. 7. Data retention We keep your data for as long as your account is active. After you request deletion, data is purged within 30 days (the grace window gives you a chance to recover the account). Backups are rotated on a rolling 7-day window and contain no separately retrievable copy of purged data. 8. Children PeptideStack is not directed to children under 18.
