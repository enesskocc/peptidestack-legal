1. Who we are

PeptideStack ("the App", "we", "us") is an educational tracking journal that lets you log peptides you take, dosages, frequency, and notes — for personal reference only. The App does not provide medical advice, does not source any substance, and does not recommend any protocol.

Operator: Enes Koç, Esslingen, Germany.
Contact for privacy questions: enesskocc5@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

We only collect data you actively provide:

  • Account: email address and a hashed password (handled by Supabase Auth — we never see your plaintext password).
  • Profile: optional display name, language preference, onboarding answers (goals, experience level).
  • Your peptide stack: peptide name (from our public catalog), dosage, frequency, personal notes you choose to add.
  • Dose log entries: timestamp, dose taken, optional notes you write.
  • Subscription state: whether you have an active premium entitlement, the product identifier, and expiration date — synced from RevenueCat. We do not store payment information; that stays with Apple.
  • Account lifecycle: account creation date and, if you request deletion, the deletion-requested timestamp.

We do not use third-party analytics (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel). We do not show ads. We do not integrate Apple HealthKit. We do not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and keep your stack in sync across devices.
  • To gate access to premium features based on your subscription state.
  • To honour App Store and GDPR rights (access, deletion).

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with marketers.

4. Where your data is stored

Personal data is stored in Supabase (Postgres database, EU region — Frankfurt, Germany). Subscription state is processed by RevenueCat (purchase metadata only; no personally identifiable information beyond the user identifier we generate). Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple.

5. Third parties we rely on

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) — database & authentication. supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.) — subscription state management. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple — App Store distribution and in-app purchase processing. apple.com/legal/privacy

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Receive an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

You can exercise the deletion right at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. Your account is deactivated immediately and signs you out. After 30 days, all of your personal data is permanently and irreversibly purged from our database. If you sign in within those 30 days, you can restore the account.

For any other request, write to the contact email above. We respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. After you request deletion, data is purged within 30 days (the grace window gives you a chance to recover the account). Backups are rotated on a rolling 7-day window and contain no separately retrievable copy of purged data.

8. Children

PeptideStack is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be announced inside the App and reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

2. Welche Daten wir erheben

Wir erheben nur Daten, die du aktiv eingibst:

  • Profil: optionaler Anzeigename, Spracheinstellung, Onboarding-Antworten (Ziele, Erfahrungslevel).
  • Dein Peptid-Stack: Peptidname (aus unserem öffentlichen Katalog), Dosis, Häufigkeit, persönliche Notizen.

  • Abostatus: ob ein aktives Premium-Recht besteht, Produkt-ID, Ablaufdatum — synchronisiert von RevenueCat. Wir speichern keine Zahlungsinformationen; diese verbleiben bei Apple.
